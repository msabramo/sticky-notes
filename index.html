✨ Start a New Board

Tap + to add a note. Drag a note's colored header to move it, tap its body to type, and drag its bottom-right corner to resize it. Use the ● button to change its color. Hover over a note (or tap/select it on a touch device) to reveal its formatting toolbar: font, size, text color, bold/italic/underline/strikethrough, headings, bullet/numbered/checklist lists, links, alignment, indent, and images — select some text first to format just that part or to turn it into a link; with nothing selected, an inserted image drops in at your cursor. Tap a checklist item's checkbox to check it off. Use the 🏷 button on a note to set custom fields (status, assignee, tags, or whatever you define in Manage Fields — including a Person(s) field that auto-fills from everyone who's used this board, optionally allowing more than one person and/or a freely-typed name for someone not on the board). Tap the circle next to ☰ to set your name, initials, and color so others can tell who's who. Tap 📷 to snap a photo of a handwritten or printed to-do list and turn each item into its own note. Use "Create Columns…" to lay out a kanban-style board (e.g. To do, In progress, Done), or "Draw a Zone" to drag out your own labeled region — tap a zone's label to rename it or its × to remove it, and drag its bottom-right corner to resize it. "Create Columns from Field…" makes one column per option of a select field (e.g. Assignee) and keeps them in sync both ways: drop a note in a column and its field updates to match, or change the field on a note and it hops to that column. Dropped notes auto-stack into an open spot in whichever column or zone they land in — drop near the top, bottom, or between two notes to choose where; hold Option (Alt) while dropping to place a note freely instead. Everyone with the invite link edits this same board live. Pinch, scroll, or use the +/− buttons to zoom; drag empty space to pan.
